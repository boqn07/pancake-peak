## Shared success patterns

1. **One tactile action repeated thousands of times**, with a sound, a number and a visible result
   every time (Throw Food For Animals, Fish It).
2. **A small decision or skill moment inside the loop**: Grow a Garden's "harvest now or wait",
   Fish It's timing bar. Pure click loops churn once the novelty fades.
3. **Something visibly growing on screen**: the "+1" wave and Tiny Tumble's ball both sell growth
   you can see from across the map.
4. **Three stacked progression layers** (upgrades → zones → prestige), each zone a visual reset.
5. **Eggs/rarity collection with visible odds and a dramatic reveal**, plus followers to show off.
6. **Social pull without griefing**: shared spaces, server events, announcements of big moments.
7. **Return hooks**: daily streak, playtime gifts, codes, timed boosts.
8. **Mobile-first** controls, and monetization through speed and convenience (2x, auto, luck, slots).

## Oversaturated (avoid)

- Steal-and-base games: the dominant trend for a year, crowded, and theft invites toxicity for a
  young audience.
- Ragdoll/physics-chaos games: the newest wave, but physics-heavy and hard to make server-
  authoritative and fair in a simulator economy.
- Plain "click a breakable with your pets" loops, another fishing clone, strength simulators.
- Our own earlier games: Lumen Lasso (dusk floating islands, light-ribbon lasso), Tiny Tumble
  (thumb-sized in a house, growing ball), Pop Lagoon (bright tropical lagoon, chain-popping bubbles).
  The fourth game must not reuse their theme, verb or look.

## Gaps we can use

- **Timing-stack games** (the mobile classic "drop the block, the overhang is cut off") are proven
  fun for all ages and nearly absent from Roblox simulators. They give a skill moment on every
  action, a push-your-luck decision (serve now or go taller), and something that visibly grows.
- **A tower you carry is a social signal**: a 40-pancake tower wobbling over a player's head is
  visible from across the zone, which turns progress into show-off without trading or stealing.
- **Food service** (Sell Fries!, Grow a Garden's selling) is popular, and cute hungry creatures fit
  the "feed the animals" appeal of Throw Food For Animals.
- **Snowy alpine, cozy and warm** is unused by our three games (dusk, indoor, tropical) and reads
  instantly in a thumbnail.

## Conclusion

Build **Pancake Peak**: a snowy storybook mountain where tiny chefs balance ever-taller pancake
towers on a tray over their heads and serve them to hungry Yetis. The verb (drop timing + slicing)
and the push-your-luck height decision are new for us and rare on Roblox, while the egg collection,
zones, rebirth, events and passes follow the patterns players come back for.
